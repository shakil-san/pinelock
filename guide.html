How Pinelock works

Pinelock locks the apps that eat your time. Getting back in takes a little effort you chose, just enough to ask yourself whether it’s worth it. This guide covers everything in the app; the short version is: make a lock, pick how strict it is, and let your pine grow.

Getting started

  1. Install TestFlight, then Pinelock from your invite. You need an iPhone on iOS 16 or later.
  2. Open Pinelock and go through three short screens. When iOS asks, allow Screen Time access: it’s how Pinelock locks apps. iOS never tells Pinelock which apps you pick.
  3. Tap Try your first lock, pick one app you open without thinking, and lock it for 15 minutes. Open that app to see Pinelock step in.

A safe first lock: start Gentle, and leave out apps you may need (Messages, Maps, banking, sign-in and work apps).

Kinds of lock

  • Schedule: on at set times, like 10 PM to 7 AM, every day or on chosen days. Templates (Bedtime, Work hours, Morning focus, Weekend detox) fill in the times.
  • Daily limit (beta): the apps stay open until you’ve used them for, say, 30 minutes in total that day, then lock until midnight.
  • Until I unlock: locks straight away and stays locked until you unlock it.
  • Focus now: a one-off lock from now, for 30 minutes or longer, on Today or from the Control Center button. It deletes itself when it ends.

Each lock can also say what it protects (Sleep, Deep work, Family time, or your own words). You’ll see it when you open a locked app: “You set this lock to protect: Sleep.”

Gentle, Firm and Hard

Every lock, and every Focus now, has one strength:

  • Gentle (where everyone starts): the ways to unlock you chose, plus free passes. You get one free pass a day to start with (Settings changes this).
  • Firm: only the ways you chose. No free passes.
  • Hard: no way out inside Pinelock until the lock ends, so there are no ways to choose. You can’t edit, turn off or delete it while it’s on. Before it starts, Pinelock shows the exact end time; Focus now also offers a 15-minute try first. Hard isn’t offered for “Until I unlock”, because that has no end.

Unlocking

Open a locked app and tap Unlock with Pinelock, then the notification (or open Pinelock yourself within 5 minutes). Pinelock asks for one slow breath, then, if you like, why you need it. Then choose a way in:

  • Questions: 5 to 20 of them (10 to start), such as sums, number sequences, typing a phrase or counting letters. For sums there’s a Scratch pad to work things out with your finger.
  • Walk: 250, 500 or 1,000 steps with the phone in your pocket. Turn it on in Settings first. Walk somewhere safe, and never while driving.
  • NFC tag (beta): tap a sticker you keep somewhere inconvenient. Pair it in Settings.
  • Free pass (Gentle locks): skip the questions for one short break. In a Focus now session a pass covers at most half the time left, so it can’t empty the session.
  • Wait it out (optional, Settings → Allow waiting instead): wait 30 minutes instead of answering, with Pinelock closed if you like, then unlock within 10 minutes. Not for hard locks.

Never trapped: a lock that only allows walking or a tag also offers “the long way”, twice the usual questions on Hard, so a lost tag or a bad day never means deleting the app.

After you unlock

  • Take a break: the apps open for your break length (15, 30 or 60 minutes, set in Settings) and lock again by themselves. Lock again now ends it early.
  • Off until 7:00 AM (schedules): skips the rest of tonight’s window. The lock comes back next time by itself.
  • Off for the rest of today (daily limits): back tomorrow.
  • Turn this lock off (“Until I unlock”) or End this session (Focus now).

Two locks on one app: an app stays locked until every lock covering it allows it. If another lock is on, Pinelock tells you before you start (“Evening Focus is also on. Apps it locks stay locked after this unlock.”) and again afterwards.

Changing a lock

  • Making a lock stricter saves straight away.
  • Making a lock that’s on easier (fewer apps, less time, a weaker strength, turning it off) takes one of its unlocks, and counts as an unlock for your pine.
  • Making Settings easier while a lock is on (more passes, fewer questions, waiting) also takes an unlock, but doesn’t end your run of protected days: it opens no app. Hard locks aren’t affected while they’re on.
  • Hard locks can’t be changed until they end.

Protected days and your pine

  • A protected day is a day a lock guarded something (or you stayed under a daily limit) and you didn’t unlock early. Your pine grows a little each one, and takes a new shape at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days.
  • Free passes don’t count against it. Days with nothing locked are paused: they don’t count and don’t end the run (“12 protected days (2 paused)”).
  • An early unlock ends the run. Your pine is planted in your forest, where it stays, and a new seed starts. The urges you resisted still count.
  • The bigger milestones are something to aim for, not a test you fail.

Time saved

Tell Pinelock roughly how long you used the apps you lock before (on Progress, or when a tip asks). It compares that with the time iOS reports on those apps, in 15-minute steps, and shows an estimate per day, week, month and year after 3 measured days. “Under 1 hour” counts as 30 minutes. Days the measurement didn’t run are left out, not counted as zero. If you change which apps you lock, Pinelock measures again and asks whether your answer still fits.

Reminders and widgets

  • Reminders 5, 10 or 15 minutes before a scheduled lock starts (Settings).
  • Widgets: “Your pine” and “Today” (what’s locked and when it frees up).
  • Control Center: a Focus now button (iOS 18 or later).
  • Settings → Make Pinelock harder to remove walks through a Screen Time passcode, so stopping Pinelock takes more than a tap.

The pilot

  • The TestFlight pilot is free. Taking part doesn’t include lifetime access to future paid features. If Pinelock has paid features later, pricing and what stays free will be announced before launch.
  • What we’d like you to try: a schedule you’ll keep (bedtime is a good one), a few days of real use, and at least one unlock, so you see what happens to your pine.
  • Honest feedback helps most, including what annoyed you. Use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback (or take a screenshot), or email pinelock.support@gmail.com.
  • Android: not available, and no date is set. If you’d use it, email us your phone model and what you’d lock; it helps us decide.
